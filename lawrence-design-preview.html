What this preview is, and is not

  • Is: the real layout, hierarchy, type scale, spacing and colour. The wordmark shown is the type-only lockup described in §13.4 — display face, all caps, 0.18em tracking, second line at 38 %.
  • Is not: final photography. Images are tonal gradients standing in for imagery the generator sources automatically (§13.2). Cormorant Garamond stands in for Canela; Inter is the production body face.
  • Note what is deliberately absent: no lock icons, no blurred cards, no countdowns, no chat widget. Restriction is expressed as a quiet line and a real number.
  • Every statistic shown carries a source and a date, exactly as the specification requires — that discipline is what makes the market pages citable.
